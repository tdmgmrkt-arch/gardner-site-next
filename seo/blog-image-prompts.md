## Common failure modes we're fixing

1. **Wrong region** — snow, ice, Northern climates (Riverside County is arid Mediterranean)
2. **Wrong trade** — cast-iron radiators (SoCal is forced-air HVAC), gutter cleaning tools tagged as hydro-jetting
3. **Wrong scale** — municipal infrastructure, industrial pipe farms, city sewer manhole covers on residential posts
4. **Foreign context** — Cyrillic/European signage, painted teal walls, European bathroom fixtures
5. **Wrong subject** — drain strainer for garbage disposal, flush handle for running toilet, TP for flushable wipes
6. **Stock-photo tells** — dramatic HDR lighting, colored studio backlights, thumbs-up business people, overly perfect CGI
7. **Missing the equipment** — 3 water-heater posts didn't show a water heater
